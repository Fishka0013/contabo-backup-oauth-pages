Hermes Backup Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Purpose and data access

Hermes Backup is a private, owner-operated backup utility. It uses Google Drive's limited drive.file OAuth scope to create, list and verify its own backup files and manage files explicitly authorized by the owner. It does not request unrestricted access to the owner's Drive.

Use and storage

Backup payloads are encrypted using Restic before transfer to the authorized Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored in restricted local configuration on the backup host. Operational metadata, including backup timestamps, sizes and status, may be recorded for verification. Healthchecks receives operational success, start or failure signals, not backup payloads or OAuth credentials.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell Google user data, share it for advertising, use it for generalized AI training, or provide a public backup service. Google Drive is the selected storage provider. Retention and deletion are controlled by the owner; the current daily backup configuration retains snapshots without automatic pruning.

Security and revocation

The owner controls encryption and recovery credentials separately. Access may be revoked in Google Account third-party connections. Revoking authorization stops further access but does not itself delete previously stored backup files. The owner can independently manage or delete retained backup data in Google Drive.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
