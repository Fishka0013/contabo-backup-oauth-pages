Hermes Backup

Hermes Backup is a private, owner-operated utility for preserving encrypted backups of the owner's Hermes infrastructure. It is not a public backup service.

How it works

Restic encrypts selected backup data before rclone transfers it to the authorized Google Drive account. The utility requests the limited Google Drive drive.file scope to manage files it creates or that are explicitly opened with it, not unrestricted access to all Drive files.

Access is granted through Google's OAuth authorization flow. The owner manages recovery and the encryption passphrase separately.
