Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Purpose

Contabo Backup is a personal, owner-operated backup tool. It stores backup archives selected by the owner in a Google Drive account authorized by that same owner.

Google user data accessed

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. This grants access to Drive files created by the app and files the owner explicitly selects or opens with the app. The tool uses this access for creating, listing, checking and deleting its own backup files according to the owner's retention policy. It does not need general access to all files in the owner's Drive.

Data handling and retention

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. Operational metadata, such as file names, sizes, timestamps and success or failure of a backup, may be recorded locally for diagnostics and notifications. Backup retention is controlled by the owner; no public user accounts or third-party analytics are used.

Disclosure

The tool does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising. Encrypted backup files are stored with Google Drive as the selected storage provider. No Google user data is used to train generalized AI models.

Security and access

OAuth credentials are used solely for authorized backup operations. They are not placed in this public website or repository. Access can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google Account settings, under third-party connections.

Contact

For questions about this owner-operated tool, use the support email listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
